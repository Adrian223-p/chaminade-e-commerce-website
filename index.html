Our Mission

Building school spirit, one Griffin at a time.

Griffin Gear exists to bring the Chaminade community together through affordable, high quality spirit wear. Every purchase supports student clubs, athletics and school events, so when you wear green and gold, you’re investing right back into your school.

Run by Grade 11 Entrepreneurship students, our store is a real world classroom where we practise marketing, customer service and digital business while helping Griffins everywhere show their pride.
