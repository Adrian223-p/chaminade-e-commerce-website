Privacy & Security

Your trust matters. Here’s exactly how we protect your personal information when you shop with Griffin Gear.

🔒 Our Privacy Policy

Griffin Gear only collects the information we need to process your order — such as your name, email and product selection. We never sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone. Your data is used solely to fulfill orders, arrange pickup and improve our store.

💳 Secure Payment Methods

All online payments are processed through encrypted, PCI-compliant payment providers using SSL/TLS encryption. We accept cash and debit in person, and secure cash-online transfers. Griffin Gear staff never see or store your full card or banking details.

🛡️ Password Protection

If you create a store account, your password is encrypted (hashed) and can’t be read by anyone — not even us. We recommend a strong, unique password and never sharing your login. Accounts automatically lock after several failed attempts to keep them safe.

🍪 Use of Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to remember what’s in your cart and to understand which products are popular. These cookies do not store payment details. You can disable cookies in your browser at any time and still browse the store.

🗄️ Customer Information Storage

Order information is stored on secure, password-protected school systems and is accessible only to authorized Griffin Gear staff and our supervising teacher. We keep records only as long as needed to complete and support your order, then they are safely deleted.

🤝 Third-Party Services

We work with a small number of trusted partners — our payment processor and apparel supplier — strictly to complete your order. These partners follow their own privacy and security standards and are never permitted to use your data for marketing.

✅ Safe Online Shopping Practices

  • Always check for the padlock 🔒 and https:// in the address bar.
  • Only shop on the official Griffin Gear site linked from the school.
  • Never share your password or payment details over email or text.
  • Log out when using a shared or school computer.
  • Keep your browser and device software up to date.
  • Report anything suspicious to a teacher or store staff right away.

Questions about your privacy?

Email us at store@chaminade-griffins.ca — we’re happy to explain how your information is handled.
