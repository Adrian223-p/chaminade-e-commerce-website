About Griffin Gear

How we started, who we serve, and the promise we make to every customer.

Our Story

How the store was created

Griffin Gear began as a Grade 11 Entrepreneurship project with a simple observation: students wanted an easy, affordable way to buy Chaminade spirit wear, but the only option was a once-a-year paper order form that was easy to miss. Sizes sold out, designs were limited, and there was no place to see everything in one spot.

So we built an online school store. We researched what students actually wear, sourced quality blanks, and partnered with the school to put our three official logos — the crest, the griffin and the eagle — on every product. What started as a class assignment became a real, working storefront for the whole Griffin community.

What problem do we solve?

Our store meets a real need (accessible, year-round spirit wear and gear) and a real want (looking good while repping your school). It gives students one trusted place to browse, compare and order — and it keeps the money inside our own community by funding clubs, teams and events.

Who We Serve

Our Target Market

Griffin Gear is built for the whole Chaminade community — but we know exactly who our customers are.

🎒 Students

Our core customers — Grades 9–12 who want comfortable, stylish gear to wear at school, at games and on spirit days.

🍎 Staff & Teachers

Faculty and coaches who represent the school every day and want quality apparel like sweaters, jackets and hats.

👪 Parents & Families

Supportive parents buying gifts, game-day wear and bottles or patches to cheer on their Griffins.

🎓 Alumni

Graduates who want to stay connected to Chaminade and proudly wear the green and gold for years to come.

🏘️ Community Members

Neighbours and local supporters who attend school events and want to show their pride for the Griffins.

🏆 Sports Teams & Clubs

A specific group: teams and clubs ordering matching gear, footballs, patches and bottles in bulk.

Our Promise

Customer Service Policy

We want every Griffin to shop with confidence. Here’s how we take care of you.

↩️ Returns & Refunds

Unworn items in original condition may be returned within 14 days of pickup with your receipt for a full refund or store credit. Custom-printed names and personalized varsity jackets are final sale.

🔁 Exchanges

Wrong size? No problem. We’ll exchange any unworn apparel for a different size or colour within 30 days, subject to availability — free of charge.
